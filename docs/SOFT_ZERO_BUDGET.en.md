# Soft Zero Budget – complete guide

Valid for BudgetManager 2.2.43.

Open **File → Settings → Behaviour → Budget overview** and enable **Soft Zero Budget**. The formula is **income − expenses − savings ≈ CHF 0**. “Soft” means suggestions only: no automatic booking, moving or budget change.

A positive income budget is required. The target month's plan is checked first; a stable pattern from completed previous months may be used when the plan is already nearly balanced. Tiny rounding differences are ignored.

For a surplus, choose to increase savings or show carryover. For a deficit, savings are suggested first and then flexible expenses. Fixed/recurring costs, pots/reserves and incremental annual costs remain protected.

Example surplus: CHF 5,000 income − CHF 3,000 expenses − CHF 1,000 savings = CHF 1,000 to assign. Example deficit: CHF 5,000 income − CHF 4,000 expenses − CHF 1,500 savings = CHF 500 deficit; savings are suggested down to CHF 1,000.

This is different from tracking learning, which creates starting budgets where none exist. Soft Zero Budget checks the whole plan. Month-end close works with actual completed months; this feature remains a planning rule.

Troubleshooting: save the setting, verify a positive income budget, select the correct month/year, allow enough completed history, and open the suggestions view. Fixed costs not being cut is intentional.
